Disability statistics and context

Disability statistics depend on definitions, measures, populations, and collection methods. Figures from different studies should not be treated as directly comparable or as a description of any individual person.

This study tool does not publish standalone population statistics. To keep the study content aligned with the IAAP materials selected for this site, use the CPACC Body of Knowledge, certification content outline, and sample exam questions linked on the homepage for authoritative study context.

My data and privacy

Saved attempts are stored in local browser storage only. Export a backup before clearing browser data or moving to another device. Export files contain your scores, answers, and attempt dates.

Import validates the file and merges valid attempts with saved attempts. No data is uploaded. This site makes no analytics or results-collection requests.
